Why some settings cannot convert directly

Universal power controls pulse duty cycle. The 60W-to-80W ratio does not establish equivalent measured output. Universal speed percentages depend on the motion system and process; acceleration and geometry affect actual speed. Vector PPI describes pulses along a path, while raster lines/cm describes spacing between scan lines.

This calculator does not match spot size, pulse behavior, focus, air assist, passes, or material response. It does not automatically increase speed to compensate for higher rated power.
